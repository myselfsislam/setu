Setu is a free money planner for Indians moving to the UK.

Common questions

Where is my data?

Only on your device. Use Settings → Back up your data to save a copy, and restore it on a new phone.

My month starts on pay day. Can Setu do that?

Yes. Settings → Your month starts on, then pick your pay day.

Some India payments come from my Indian account.

In Spending → Monthly costs, set “Paid from” to “From Indian account”. They won’t be taken from your UK money.

Is Setu financial advice?

No. It gives general guidance and simple calculations. Talk to a qualified adviser before big decisions.
