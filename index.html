Setu: a free money planner for Indians moving to the UK

Setu helps you manage money across India and the UK. It's free, takes 3 minutes to set up, and needs no sign-up. Everything you enter stays on your device.

  • See your UK take-home pay each month, in pounds and Indian rupees (2026/27 tax rates, including Scotland).
  • Track your spending against simple budgets, with a month-end forecast.
  • Follow a first-90-days checklist for both countries: NRO and NRE accounts, National Insurance, tax code, council tax, ISA and pension.
  • Check the hidden cost of sending money from the UK to India.
  • Plan a home and retirement back in India.
