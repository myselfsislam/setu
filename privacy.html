Privacy policy

Last updated 2 October 2026. Applies to the Setu website and the Setu apps for iPhone and Android.

In short: Setu doesn’t collect your personal data. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. What you enter stays on your device.

What you enter

Your salary, costs, loans, spending, goals and checklist progress are stored only on your device (in the browser, or in the app’s private storage). They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app, or using Settings → Start over, removes them.

Sync across devices (optional)

If you sign in to sync, we store your email address (to send sign-in codes) and an encrypted copy of your Setu data with our hosting provider, Supabase. Your data is encrypted on your device with a passphrase only you know, before it’s uploaded, so neither Setu nor Supabase can read it. You can sign out at any time; to delete your synced copy and account, contact us through the support page.

Backups and sharing

If you download a backup or export spending as a CSV file, the file is saved or shared wherever you choose. We never see it.

Public data Setu fetches

To show live figures, your device requests public data directly from: Frankfurter and ExchangeRate-API (exchange rates), mfapi.in (Indian fund prices), and a headlines file on the Setu website (built from Google News and GOV.UK). These requests contain none of your Setu data. Like any website, those services can see your device’s IP address.

Visit counts (website only)

The website counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and collects no personal data. The apps don’t count visits.

Face ID, fingerprint and reminders (apps)

If you turn on the lock, your phone checks your face, fingerprint or passcode. Setu never receives biometric data. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent to a server.

Links to other websites

Some links, including clearly labelled referral links, open other websites with their own privacy policies. Setu doesn’t share anything with them.

Children

Setu is meant for adults managing their own money.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on GitHub or see the support page.
