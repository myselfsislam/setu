UK tax on your Indian income: the 4-year relief

Once you're UK resident, the UK can tax your income from India too. New arrivals may get a 4-year break, but it has to be claimed and it isn't always worth it.

In short

  • UK residents are taxed on their worldwide income and gains, including NRO and NRE interest, rent from a flat in India, and gains on Indian mutual funds.
  • If you hadn't lived in the UK for the 10 tax years before you arrived, you may be able to claim relief on foreign income and gains for your first 4 tax years here.
  • It isn't automatic. You claim it each year on a Self Assessment tax return.

Who can claim

The foreign income and gains (FIG) regime started on 6 April 2025 and replaced the old non-dom rules. You qualify in your first four tax years of UK residence if you weren't UK resident in any of the previous ten tax years. It's based on residence, not nationality. Your UK salary is taxed as normal; the relief only covers foreign income and gains.

The catch: you lose your tax-free allowances

In any year you claim, you give up your £12,570 Personal Allowance and your Capital Gains Tax annual exempt amount. For someone on a full UK salary with modest interest in India, that can cost more than the relief saves. It tends to help people with large income or gains in India. Work it out both ways, or ask a tax adviser, before claiming.

How to claim

  1. Register for Self Assessment with HMRC if you haven't before. If you became UK resident in a tax year, register by 5 October after that tax year ends.
  2. Fill in the residence and foreign income pages of your tax return, listing the foreign income and gains you want relief on.
  3. Claim within the time limit: for 2025/26, the deadline is 31 January 2028.

Don't forget India

Income earned in India can still be taxed in India, for example NRO interest has tax deducted at source. The UK–India tax treaty helps avoid paying twice on the same income. Keep records of Indian tax paid.

Common questions

Do I pay UK tax on NRE interest?

It can be taxable in the UK because UK residents are taxed on worldwide income, even though India doesn't tax NRE interest. New arrivals may be able to claim the 4-year relief.

Who qualifies for the 4-year FIG relief?

People in their first four tax years of UK residence who weren't UK resident in any of the ten tax years before they arrived.

Is the 4-year relief automatic?

No. You claim it each year on a Self Assessment tax return. If you claim, you lose your Personal Allowance and Capital Gains Tax annual exempt amount for that year.

Is claiming always worth it?

Not always. If your income from India is small, losing your £12,570 Personal Allowance can cost more than the tax you save.
