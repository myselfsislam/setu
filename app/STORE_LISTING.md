## Description
Moving from India to the UK? Setu is a free, private money planner built for you.

• See your UK take-home pay and exactly what's left each month, in pounds and rupees
• Track spending by category, set budgets, and start your month on pay day
• Keep EMIs, SIPs and family payments in India in one place, whether they're paid from UK money or your Indian account
• Follow a first-90-days checklist for both countries, with ready-to-send emails for your bank, lenders, council and HR
• Plan a home and retirement back in India, and compare fund types with their past performance
• Check what your bank really charges to send money home
• Lock the app with Face ID or fingerprint, and get pay-day reminders

No sign-up. No ads. No tracking. Everything stays on your phone.

Setu gives general guidance, not financial advice.

## App privacy answers
- **Apple "App Privacy": Data Not Collected.** The app has no accounts, analytics or ads, and stores data only on the device. Public data requests (exchange rates, fund prices, headlines) carry no user data.
- **Google "Data safety":** No data collected; no data shared. Data is not encrypted in transit *because none is sent*; answer "No" to collection. Users can delete data in the app (Settings → Start over).
- **Google financial features declaration:** The app provides budgeting and financial information only. It does not offer loans, trading, banking or payments.

## Review notes (paste into App Review / Play review)
Setu works fully offline and needs no login. Face ID is optional (Settings → Lock with Face ID or fingerprint). Reminders are optional local notifications (Settings → Monthly reminders). Referral links, if shown, are labelled and open in the browser.

*Why this isn't "just a website":* Apple can reject apps that only wrap a website (guideline 4.2). Setu adds offline use, private on-device storage, Face ID lock, local pay-day reminders, the native share sheet for backups and CSV exports, and haptics.
